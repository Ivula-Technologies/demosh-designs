Demosh Designs is a design and print studio in Kamutiini, Lower Kabete, led by Gordon Omala.

Our story

We bring ideas to life.

Demosh Designs is your destination for quality apparel, professional branding, personalised gifts and printing services.

Whether it is a T-shirt for your team, a new look for your business or a gift for someone special, we treat every order as a chance to make something people are proud to wear, use and give. And because ideas don't keep office hours, neither do we: Demosh is open 24 hours.

Creativity

Every piece starts with your idea. We help shape it into a design that stands out.

Quality

Good materials and careful production, so what you get looks right and lasts.

Attention to detail

Colours, spelling, placement and finish are checked before anything leaves the studio.
